A quick note about your score

Your Vitamova score is an estimate based only on your activity inside the app. It is designed to help guide your practice, not to replace a full language evaluation.

1

Strong vocabulary recognition is important, but real language ability also includes how smoothly you speak and how naturally you can put ideas together.

2

Contextual understanding matters too. A learner’s level depends on how well they understand words, sentences, tone, and meaning in real situations.

3

Recall speed is part of fluency. The faster you can recognize, remember, and use language comfortably, the more natural your communication becomes.
