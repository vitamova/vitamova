Level transparency

How Vitamova measures your level

Vitamova does not treat your level as a simple score. Instead, it looks at evidence from your activity: the questions you answer, the words you review, and the vocabulary ranges where you are most ready to grow.

The two ranges Vitamova targets

Vitamova uses your activity to target vocabulary questions in two important areas. The first area is the range where you are closest to mastery. In this system, mastery means knowing about 80% or more of the words in a 1,000-word range. The second area is your learning edge.

Closest to mastery

This is the range where you appear closest to proving mastery. Questions here help Vitamova confirm whether you are ready to advance your level.

Learning edge

This is the range where Vitamova estimates you are most likely to achieve meaningful growth from vocabulary development. It is based on statistical models of language knowledge, your app activity, and your performance.

These two ranges are related, but they are not always the same. One range may help confirm what you have already mastered, while the other helps the app find the most productive area for new learning.

Mastery confidence and coverage

Vitamova uses two different measurements to understand your level. They answer related but different questions.

Mastery confidence

Mastery confidence is the app’s confidence that you have achieved mastery of a given 1,000-word range. Here, mastery means that the evidence suggests you know about 80% or more of the words in that range.

Coverage

Coverage measures how much of that range you have actually covered by learning and reviewing words inside Vitamova. If you only started using Vitamova recently, your coverage numbers are likely to be very low even if you already know many words outside the app.

You can raise your level in two ways: by increasing mastery confidence through correct answers in the Vocabulary Builder, or by increasing coverage as you learn and review more words over time.

Why the numbers may look conservative

Vitamova is careful about level measurement because your level is meant to represent a demonstrated foundation, not a guess about everything you might know. You may already know more than the app has measured. As you answer more questions and review more words, Vitamova gathers stronger evidence and can raise your level with more confidence.

In practical terms, this means your level should become more accurate over time. Early numbers may look modest, especially if your coverage is low, but continued activity gives the system more evidence about where you are already strong and where you are ready to grow next.
